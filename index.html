Information available for each proposal

Each proposal may include the following information to help the public understand the subject under discussion.

  • Proposal title and summary.
  • Proposing institution or party.
  • Objectives and regulatory scope.
  • Draft documents and supporting academic paper.
  • Consultation schedule and feedback deadline.
  • Discussion status and follow-up.

Tips for writing effective input
  • Describe the issue specifically.
  • Explain its impact on the community.
  • Offer a realistic suggestion within the policy-maker’s authority.
  • Use clear and respectful language.
